Some things I've been wanting to tell you. Turn your sound on, and scroll gently.

her energy

You carry your own kind of sunshine. There are people who walk into a room and simply exist, and then there are people like you who walk into a room and somehow change its temperature. You have this beautiful way of making people smile, of bringing warmth where there was none, and of reminding people that there is still something good to laugh about. Never underestimate the light you carry. The world is genuinely brighter because you are in it.

to a daughter of the earth

May you always remember that you are a part of something much bigger than yourself. May the earth beneath your feet remind you to stay grounded. May the sun on your skin remind you that you deserve warmth. May the ocean remind you that it is okay to have depths. May the trees remind you that growth does not have to be rushed. You are nature too. You are allowed to shed old versions of yourself, grow through difficult seasons, bloom unexpectedly, and begin again. Just like the earth, you were never meant to remain the same.

the seasons no one saw

There were days when simply getting through the day was an achievement. There were moments when the light felt far away, and seasons that asked more of you than anyone should ever have to give. But somehow, you kept going. You found your way back to yourself. And look at you now. Still laughing. Still loving. Still dreaming. Still becoming. Not because life was always gentle with you, but because somewhere inside you, there was a strength that refused to disappear. I hope you know how incredibly proud I am of the woman who made it here.

her new chapter

Thirty is not an ending. It is a beginning. May this decade meet you softly. May you find the kind of peace you don't have to fight for. May you experience love that feels safe, friendships that feel like home, mornings that make you grateful to be alive, and dreams that scare you because they're bigger than anything you've imagined before. May you stop shrinking yourself for places that were never big enough for you. May you take up space. May you laugh loudly. May you love deeply. May you travel far. May you heal completely. And may the next ten years be proof that there was so much more waiting for you on the other side of everything you've survived.

a toast

So here's to you. Here's to the little girl you once were. Here's to the woman you've become. Here's to every version of you that had to exist for you to arrive here. Here's to the tears, the laughter, the lessons, the mistakes, the healing, the adventures, the people you've loved and the lives you've touched. Here's to everything you've survived. Here's to everything you've yet to experience. And most importantly — here's to 30. May this be the decade where you don't just survive. May you absolutely, unapologetically live.
